03 — Choreographic Interventions
BODY

SPATIAL CHOREOGRAPHY

Designing movement scores that operate beyond the stage — infiltrating galleries, streets, institutions, and digital space as sites of performative potential.

BODY AS ARCHIVE

The body retains collective histories, traumas, and possibilities. Jaskaran's practice excavates these somatic archives through durational and participatory methodologies.

RITUAL SYSTEMS

Drawing from trans-cultural performance traditions to construct new ritual frameworks that honor complexity, contradiction, and communal becoming.
