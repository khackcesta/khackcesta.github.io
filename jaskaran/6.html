§ 001 — ARTISTIC MANIFESTO

THEEMBODIEDCITY
"What happens when Goddess Kali meets Kaiserin Sisi? When the body becomes the city, and the city becomes the body?"
Jaskaran Anand is a trans* artist, performer, and cultural disruptor working at the intersection of identity, urban space, and collective memory. Through performance, installation, and living archive, their practice investigates how bodies navigate systems — institutional, urban, emotional.

Trans(T*).DisCity / City Voices is a socio-cultural initiative reimagining what it means to exist in public space — to be seen, heard, and felt.

The work refuses categorization. It is non-performative in its politics and deeply visceral in its aesthetics. It belongs to the street, the stage, the body, the void.

KALI

↳ Featured Work — April 2024

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN GODDESS KALI MEETS KAISERIN SISI

A performance intervention exploring the collision of mythologies — the Hindu goddess of destruction and transformation meeting the melancholic Austrian empress. Bodies as contested sites of colonial memory, feminine power, and cultural hybridity.

Performed at Kurdirektion Buchhandlung as part of Trans(T*).DisCity / City Voices — a socio-cultural initiative reimagining queer urban space in Vienna and Berlin.

ECHOESOFIDENTITY

001

Body as Archive

The body holds what the city forgets. Performance as memory, flesh as document, breath as resistance against erasure.

002

Urban Mythology

Ancient goddesses walking through contemporary cities. The sacred and the urban in collision — producing new mythologies of survival.

003

Trans*formation

Transition as an ongoing artistic practice. The self as medium. The community as canvas. Collective transformation as the highest art form.
