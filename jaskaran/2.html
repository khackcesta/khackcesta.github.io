Born in New Delhi (1992), Jaskaran Anand has been based in Linz, Austria since 2015 — an internationally acclaimed artist working at the volatile intersection of performance, science, and participatory art.

"The human body as an interface — between self and society, between knowing and not knowing."

Holding a Bachelor in Computer Science from University of Delhi and a combined degree in Dance Performance & Dance Pedagogy from Anton Bruckner University Linz, Jaskaran fuses both worlds: algorithmic thinking meets embodied knowledge.

He is co-founder and chairperson of trivium — a Linz-based art, cultural & research organization focused on unravelling the cultural intersectionality present in human bodies. His works span Austria, New Zealand, and India.

Currently touring as collaborator performer with Italian cultural association Chiasma/Salvo Lombardo and German choreographer Dr. Sandra Chatterjee on projects relating to identity, post-colonialism & racism.

04 — Research

Philosophy
of the
Self

◎
Neuroscience & Self
Drawing on Antonio Damasio's neuroscientific hypothesis on the working of the Self — how consciousness forms reality in a space-time continuum.
⊕
Advaita Vedanta & Identity
Referencing Mead's True.Self & False.Self, linked to Advaita Vedanta, the concept of Avataar, and quantum mechanics theory into a unified philosophy.
◈
Body as Interface
The human body as an interface for reality-formation: how sensation & perception with environmental objects shapes individual reality.
⟳
Collective Reunification
Conscious re-unification of the collective.self — investigating factors that may segregate an individual into a solipsistic reality of its own.
◻
Socio-Political Dimensions
Deconstructing reality-formation while investigating post-colonialism, racism, and the socio-political dimensions of cultural intersectionality.
∞
Trans-disciplinary Media
Using dance, video, installation, and participatory practice — always centering the human body as both subject and medium of research.
