About Jaskaran

Photo: Max Biskup
Born 1992 · New Delhi

Performance-Maker.
Researcher.
Pedagogue.

Born in New Delhi, India (1992), Jaskaran Anand has been based in Linz, Austria since 2015. His works are trans-disciplinary interventions born at the intersection of performative art, scientific and art-based research, and participatory-collaborative artistic practices — using choreography and dance as a method of communication in and beyond traditional art and public spaces.

An internationally acclaimed artist working as a performance-maker, initiator, dancer-choreographer, pedagogue, researcher, and video dramaturge-editor. He is co-founder and chairperson of trivium — a Linz-based art, cultural and research organization focused on unravelling the cultural intersectionality present in human bodies.

He holds a Bachelor of Computer Science from University of Delhi and a combined degree in Dance Performance & Dance Pedagogy from Anton Bruckner University, Linz. His works have been presented across Austria, New Zealand and India, seen at Imagetanz Festival/brut Wien, ImPulsTanz Vienna, ARS Electronica, Oriente Occidente, and Rome Europa Festival.

Fascinated by spirituality and the neuroscientific understanding of the self — drawing on Antonio Damasio, Georg H. Mead's True/False Self identities, Advaita Vedanta, and quantum mechanics — Jaskaran's research deconstructs reality-formation within the socio-political dimension surrounding it.

Research Philosophy

"The body is a site of becoming — where spirituality, neuroscience and socio-political reality collide and reform the Self."

Jaskaran's research is deeply invested in deconstructing the philosophical concept of the Self. Fascinated by the nature of spirituality, the neuro-psychological understanding of the self, and the socio-political society that surrounds it.

He refers to the neuroscientific hypothesis on the working of the Self by Antonio Damasio, Georg H. Mead's psychological concepts of True.Self & False.Self-identities — related to the Indian philosophy of Advaita Vedanta, the concept of Avataar, and quantum mechanics theory.

In his interdisciplinary works, he uses various artistic media, always focusing on the human body as the primary interface through which these themes are embodied, challenged and transformed.

Teaching & Workshops

Workshop 01
Moving Energies / Transcending Ego(s)
Body-Conscious Improvisation

A somatic and improvisational movement practice exploring the ego's role in restricting bodily expression. Taught at ImPulsTanz Vienna (since 2019), PANGEA Linz, Furore Festival and Anton Bruckner University.

Workshop 02
City Swarming: A Dance Marathon
Urban Participatory Practice

A public-space dance marathon that transforms the city into a collective body. Participants navigate, gather and disperse — mapping the invisible choreography of urban life through sustained movement.

Workshop 03
Playful Bodies
Play-Based Movement Research

Using play as a methodology to unlock pre-conditioned bodily habits. An invitation to rediscover spontaneity, vulnerability and presence through structured improvisational scores.

✦

Since 2017, Jaskaran has led movement body-conscious improvisation workshops across Europe and India — at Public Moves (ImPulsTanz Vienna), PANGEA Werkstatt der Kulturen Linz, Furore Festival (Academy of Performing Arts Baden-Württemberg), and on the streets of Linz itself.
