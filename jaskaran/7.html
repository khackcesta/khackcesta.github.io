PERFORMANCE-MAKER / RESEARCHER / PEDAGOGUE

Jaskaran Anand

Born in New Delhi and based in Linz, Jaskaran Anand works as a performer, choreographer, researcher and educator. His trans-disciplinary practice uses choreography, dance and the human body as living interfaces for questions of self, reality, culture and public space.

Artist Profile

Jaskaran Anand has lived in Linz since 2015. His work combines performative art, scientific and art-based research, and participatory collaborative practice. He has presented work across Austria, New Zealand and India, and has appeared in contexts including imagetanz / brut Wien, ImPulsTanz, Ars Electronica, Oriente Occidente, Tanztage Wien and Romaeuropa Festival.
