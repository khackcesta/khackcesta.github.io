Born (1992) in New Delhi, India. Living in Linz since 2015. Trans-disciplinary interventions at the intersections of performative-art, scientific & art-based research, and participatory-collaborative artistic practices.

An internationally acclaimed artist working as a performance-maker, dancer-choreographer, pedagogue, researcher, and video dramaturge-editor — using choreography & dance as method of communication in— & beyond— traditional art and public spaces.

Co-founder and chairperson of trivium — a Linz-based art, cultural & research organisation unravelling the cultural intersectionality present in human bodies.

Research

Dance
Is
Data

"Deconstructing the philosophical concept(s) of Self — fascinated by spirituality, the neuroscientific & psychological understandings of the self & socio-political society."

Research orientates in the conscious re-unification of the collective.self — investigating factors that may segregate an individual into a reality of its own, due to interaction, sensation & perception with objects present in its environment.

References: Antonio Damasio's neuroscientific hypothesis; Georg H. Mead's True.Self & False.Self; Indian philosophy of Advaita Vedanta; the concept of Avataar & quantum mechanics.
