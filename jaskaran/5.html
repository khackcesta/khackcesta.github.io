The Artist

THE
PRACTICE
LIVES
HERE

"His works are trans-disciplinary interventions based on intersections between performative-art, scientific & art-based research, and participatory-collaborative artistic practices using choreography & dance as communication in and beyond traditional art and public spaces."

Jaskaran Anand is a Vienna-based interdisciplinary artist whose practice extends across choreography, performance, pedagogy, and research. His work inhabits the liminal spaces between the body and space, tradition and speculation, the individual and the collective.

Drawing from Bharatanatyam, contemporary dance, somatic practices, and participatory art, Jaskaran's interventions challenge conventional boundaries — between stage and street, performer and audience, the artistic and the scientific.

Core Philosophy

WHERE
BODY
MEETS
SPACE

01
Performative Research

The body as primary research instrument. Knowledge produced through movement, sensation, and embodied experience rather than pure abstraction. Practice is theory in motion.

02
Trans-Disciplinary Intervention

Crossing borders between art, science, pedagogy, and public engagement. Each work operates in the intersection, never settling within a single discipline's grammar or institution.

03
Participatory Practice

Audience as co-creator. Community as choreographic system. The work is fundamentally incomplete without the living presence and embodied contribution of others.

04
Body-Space Dialogue

Every environment contains choreographic potential. Architecture, memory, cultural ritual, and urban infrastructure are movement scores waiting to be activated by living bodies.

CHOREOGRAPHIC RESEARCH LABORATORY

An intensive trans-disciplinary laboratory for artists, researchers, and educators working with movement, body, and space. Participants explore choreography as a method of inquiry — discovering how physical practice can generate knowledge, challenge assumptions, and open new forms of collective understanding. The lab combines somatic practices, improvisation scores, lecture-performances, and artistic research methodologies.
